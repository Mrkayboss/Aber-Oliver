About Your Midwife

Hello, I'm Aber Oliver, CM

I've spent the last two years supporting families through pregnancy, birth, and the tender months that follow. My approach blends clinical training with unhurried, personal care — I believe every birth story deserves patience, informed choices, and a steady hand to hold.

Alongside midwifery, I offer bedside nursing care for mothers and newborns who need extra support at home during recovery, so healing can happen in familiar, comfortable surroundings.

  • Certified Midwife (CM), ACNM Board Certified
  • Registered Nurse, Maternal-Newborn Specialty
  • Certified Lactation Consultant (IBCLC)
  • Neonatal Resuscitation Program (NRP) Certified

Prenatal Care

Regular checkups, screenings, and unhurried conversations to track your health and your baby's growth.

Labor & Delivery Support

Hands-on support during labor at home, birth center, or hospital — helping you feel informed and in control.

Postpartum Recovery Care

Home visits and check-ins through the fourth trimester, supporting your physical and emotional healing.

Newborn & Lactation Support

Newborn wellness checks plus breastfeeding and feeding guidance from a certified lactation consultant.

Bedside Nursing Care

In-home nursing support for mothers and newborns — wound care, vitals monitoring, medication support, and recovery care delivered right where you're most comfortable: home.

Family Planning & Reproductive Health

Confidential counseling on contraception, fertility, and reproductive wellness at every life stage.

Prenatal Education Classes

Small-group workshops on childbirth, newborn care, and early breastfeeding to help you feel ready.
